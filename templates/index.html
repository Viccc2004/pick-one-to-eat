How it works
  • Where Name a place ("in Boston") to search there. Otherwise it searches near your current location — your browser will ask. With no place and location off, it asks where; it never guesses.
  • When Say when you'll eat ("now", "Friday 7pm"). Meals count as {% for meal, time in meal_times.items() %}{{ meal }} {{ time }}{% if not loop.last %}, {% endif %}{% endfor %}.
  • Budget Say "cheap", "mid-priced" or "fancy" and places at that price come first; others appear only to make up three, and are marked. Say nothing and price is ignored.
  • Questions It may ask up to {{ max_questions }} follow-up questions. After that it stops asking: no time means now. It still needs a place.
  • Open then Places closed at that time are skipped. If a place's hours are unknown, it says so — call ahead.
  • Only real facts The reason uses only what Google returns: rating, number of reviews, price level and hours. Nothing about dishes, atmosphere or wait times.
